# Team references and availability boundary

Bellibing provides source-reviewed Wuthering Waves Character, team and rotation reference information. Reference compositions and source-based rotations are **descriptive game/reference data**, not ranked recommendations for an account's available roster.

## Available public information

- [Team composition references](ALTERNATIVE_TEAM_CONTEXTS.md) retain reviewed source identity, members, modes and limitations.
- [Team-aware rotation references](TEAM_AWARE_ROTATION_REFERENCES.md) and [composition/timing constraints](TEAM_ROTATION_COMPOSITION.md) distinguish source sequences, coordination and unresolved timing/state dependencies.
- Canonical Character, Weapon, Echo, Sonata and effect facts remain owned by the public source catalogs. Reference Team 01 is Augusta / Iuno / The Shorekeeper; this source identity does not establish complete executable team DPS.

## Execution and product availability

Arbitrary-team damage comparison, an account-roster recommendation, and multi-team allocation **are not available public features**. Published reference teams must not be presented as verified optima, and missing buffs, triggers, resource states or event timings must not be assumed to exist or treated as zero.

Supported isolated mechanics and source-backed composition facts must remain distinguishable from executable profile/whole-team DPS. When required inputs or modeling evidence are missing, the result is **Pending/Unknown**; no browser-side fallback evaluator is authorized.

See [current implementation status](PROJECT_STATUS.md), [public product contract](PRODUCT_CONTRACT.md), [damage/execution coverage](DPS_EXECUTION_GAP_MATRIX.md), and [source verification policy](DATA_VERIFICATION_POLICY.md).
