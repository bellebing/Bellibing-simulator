### Presentations and real source disagreements (not concealed)

**Adjacent Lv90 integer presentation, not stat rewrite.** Both **Encore** and **Mortefi** show DEF **+1** on Prydwen individual cards and Wuwa Wiki compared to the 2026-10-08 Prydwen roster: Encore `1247` vs **`1246`**, Mortefi `1137` vs **`1136`**. Independently published decimal progression in [Encore/Combat](https://wutheringwaves.fandom.com/wiki/Encore/Combat) (**1246.64**) and [Mortefi/Combat](https://wutheringwaves.fandom.com/wiki/Mortefi/Combat) (**1136.65**) accounts for adjacent integer displays. Encore's progression also reports **10512.50 HP**, and [Wuthering.gg Encore](https://wuthering.gg/characters/encore) shows 10512, whereas Prydwen roster/profile/Wuwa Wiki show **10513**. Zhezhi's [Lv90 decimal progression](https://wutheringwaves.fandom.com/wiki/Zhezhi/Combat) shows DEF **1197.76**; [Wuthering.gg Zhezhi](https://wuthering.gg/characters/zhezhi) truncates to **1197**, whereas Prydwen roster/profile/Wuwa Wiki show **1198**. These are explicitly distinguished **source presentation** differences, not permission to create a general rounding formula. Canonical values remain the roster integers unchanged.

**Competing named energy fields.** [ArabWuwa Encore](https://arabwuwa.com/characters/encore/), [Mortefi](https://arabwuwa.com/characters/mortefi/), [Taoqi](https://arabwuwa.com/characters/taoqi/), and [Zhezhi](https://arabwuwa.com/characters/zhezhi/) each print **`Max Energy 100`**; [Wuthering.gg Encore](https://wuthering.gg/characters/encore), [Mortefi](https://wuthering.gg/characters/mortefi), [Taoqi](https://wuthering.gg/characters/taoqi) and [Zhezhi](https://wuthering.gg/characters/zhezhi) each print **`Max Resonance Energy 140`**. Both differ **under ostensibly cap-named fields**, unlike a mere Liberation-cost comparison. These alternatives are explicitly recorded, **not treated as interchangeable or blindly dismissed as cost values**.

**Source judgment (2026-10-11):** for all four, the **current standardized Prydwen roster**, its separately named individual-character cap, and the independently titled **Wuthering Waves Wiki Max Resonance Energy** table consistently agree on **125**. The dissenting surfaces apply the same **100** and **140** values respectively across all four despite their differing character identities; neither provides a reviewed underlying data-version/field mapping proving its cap label. Given the explicit triple source-family agreement for the same cap semantic, the recorded decision retains the existing **125** and signs off these four raw records. The conflict is **not erased**: reopen source-validity review if new primary/version-pinned game evidence supports either rival interpretation. This outcome does **not** resolve Denia's separate [Worker 13 #264](https://github.com/bellebing/Bellibing-simulator/pull/264) Max Energy blocker, because that record lacks current Prydwen roster/profile corroboration.
