## Exact source facts and unresolved execution

The original source review distinguished Echo *attack alternatives*, not automatically repeated hits of one cast. Historic evidence included:

- [Sentry Construct attack descriptions](https://wuthering.wiki/monster_330000120.html): the frontal strike and charged aerial dive are separate attack variants. Selected variant, owner and landed-hit count need explicit event proof.
- [Additional Echo descriptions](https://wuwa.wiki/en/codex/echoes/60002005), [Reminiscence: Denia reference](https://wuthering.gg/echos/reminiscence-denia), [related Echo source entry](https://wuwa.wiki/en/codex/echoes/60001985) and [a second inspected attack description](https://wuthering.wiki/monster_330000200.html). These are historical supporting references, not unconditional executable attack authorizations.
- [Sanhua's source wording](https://wuthering.wiki/character_1102.html) was checked for Basic Attack amplification. A single reviewed transfer must not be generalized to unrelated effects, owners, scopes or stacking rules.

Source identity, exact Rank-5 attack component, selected variant and damage-hit count remain different from **cast occurrence**, target state, active window, rotation timing and proc qualification. Source-backed numeric amounts must be read from their current canonical game-data/effect catalogs and compared with the current review evidence; this text does not duplicate or override them.

## Reuse and profile boundary

Standalone outgoing/incoming transfers and source-qualified weapon/Echo events may be reused across eligible profiles only after checking equipment, ownership, target/recipient, timing, expiry, scope and evidence provenance. An unchanged effect amount alone does not authorize an activation or a team buff.

A `SOURCE_SEQUENCE_ONLY` rotation is not `ENGINE_MODELED` execution. Absence of an exact source-backed rotation duration or event/state history blocks a defensible DPS denominator. A source-level ability or exact-hit adapter does not remove a canonical pending profile dependency without its own independent execution proof.

The [public DPS readiness and blocker matrix](DPS_EXECUTION_GAP_MATRIX.md), [source validity review](SOURCE_VALID_EXECUTION_REVIEW_20260911.md) and `src/profileExecutionWorkQueue.ts` should be used to identify current gates and pending edges. In particular, no source-bound blocker is assumed zero or silently closed.
