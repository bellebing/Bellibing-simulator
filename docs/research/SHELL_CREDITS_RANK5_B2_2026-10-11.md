# Worker 1 — Rank-5 Echo Shell Credits and EXP carry (B2 research, 2026-10-11)

**Scope:** Only the Shell Credit cost of Rank-5 Echo upgrading/tuning from +0 through +25, including exact Tube-only EXP carry, +25 overfill, returned Tubes and integer rounding. This is **evidence and source-validity review only**; not a simulator implementation or canonical gameplay-rule promotion.

**Question:** Can [Draft PR #250, blocker B2](https://github.com/bellebing/Bellibing-simulator/pull/250) be closed for the first real `+0 → +25` simulator driver?

**Disposition: B2 = PARTIALLY VERIFIED, NOT YET CLOSED in full.** The established canonical +5…+25 *threshold costs* and the charge **on newly effective EXP rather than all supplied EXP at the +25 cap** are supported by independent current-era in-game imagery. Evidence also supports separate fixed Shell Credits per tune and no Shell Credit refund. The precise per-transaction treatment of **carried excess at an intermediate checkpoint**, actual **post-commit Tube denomination returns** at +25, and Shell Credit rounding for **non-10-multiple EXP** are not fully observed. Restricting the first driver to fresh `+0` and **Tube-only** input removes the fractional-credit case mathematically but does not prove gameplay rounding outside that scope. No unsupported rule may be promoted to close the broader blocker.

## Existing verified checkpoint contract (thresholds, not bills for arbitrary Tubes)

| Rank-5 milestone | Cumulative EXP (canonical) | Newly required EXP from preceding exact threshold | Cumulative Shell Credits incl. tunes | Ideal threshold-to-threshold Shell delta, incl. one tune |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| +0 | 0 | — | 0 | — |
| +5 | 4,400 | 4,400 | 2,440 | 2,440 |
| +10 | 16,500 | 12,100 | 5,650 | 3,210 |
| +15 | 39,600 | 23,100 | 9,960 | 4,310 |
| +20 | 79,100 | 39,500 | 15,910 | 5,950 |
| +25 | 142,600 | 63,500 | 24,260 | 8,350 |

**VERIFIED:** Table values are unchanged from `echoCoreRules.ts` and corroborated by S2/S3/S6. Each increment separates an **EXP-upgrade** amount from a **2,000-Credit tune**; reaching EXP eligibility alone does not spend 10 Tuners or 2,000 extra Credits. Thus `+0 → +25` before tuning costs 14,260 Credits; five independently performed tunes cost another 10,000 Credits. There is **no recovery of prior Shell Credits** (S2).

**Important distinction:** `+5` as a *tune-eligible level* does **not** imply current actual EXP is **exactly** 4,400. A single Gold Tube gives 5,000 EXP. Under the existing exact carry engine, the result is **5,000 cumulative EXP**, i.e. 600 already toward the next level. Charging the fixed cumulative +5 threshold `2,440` on that action rather than pricing the 5,000 accepted EXP and a **separate** tune would undercount by **60 Credits**. **The 500-Credit charge for that exact 5,000-EXP intermediate transaction is a source-rule-derived prediction (S2), not a captured +5 game screenshot.**

## Supplied vs newly accepted EXP: bounded evidence and unverified extensions

- **VERIFIED direct cap observation (S3):** 72 Purple Tube items supply 144,000 EXP; the Rank-5 cap is 142,600. The game's *displayed upgrade cost* is **14,260 Credits**. This **rejects** a blanket charge of `suppliedEXP × 0.1 = 14,400` Credits at +25. No tune cost was included in this screenshot.
- **Consistent provisional Tube-only charging interpretation:** For an upgrade step, the candidate rule is `chargedUpgradeCredits = (expAfter − expBefore) / 10` **when all quantities are divisible by ten**, with `expAfter = min(142600, expBefore + suppliedEXP)`. For a separate committed tune `chargedTuneCredits = 2000`. This agrees with the v3.7-era cap screenshot, S2's rate, and the canonical full +25 totals. **It is a hypothesis for as-yet-unmeasured individual carry steps, not a promoted canonical action contract.**
- **PENDING intermediate carry:** There is no source-paired `before balance → select 5,000 Gold at +0 → charge displayed → commit → after balance / stored 5,000 EXP` observation. S2's `500` cost-per-Gold entry favors crediting/charging full accepted 5,000 XP even though +5 became eligible at 4,400, but **the specific multi-checkpoint client transaction was not demonstrated**. Confirm also that later costs do not re-charge carried EXP.
- **PENDING overflow Tube receipt and conservation:** S3 proves a **credit preview**; it does **not** independently prove `144,000 − 142,600 = 1,400` overfill is decomposed into one Medium Tube (1,000 EXP) plus 400 unrepresented EXP when committing. That is what Bellibing's current `decomposeMaxOverflow` would do (largest first), **not an externally verified in-game inventory result**. The older S4 suggests return of excess but does not resolve the exact denomination, floor/rounding or button-repeat edge.
- **PENDING fractional rounding:** The 0.1 rate gives fractional Credits when newly accepted EXP is not divisible by 10. S2–S6 do not document `floor`, `ceil` or nearest for e.g. an effective 1-EXP increment after an EXP feed. No arbitrary-integer contract can be declared `VERIFIED` by selecting a familiar rounding mode.
- **VERIFIED scope exemption (mathematical, not client proof):** **Fresh +0 Tube-only** selected EXP is always a sum of 500/1,000/2,000/5,000; all uncapped steps are multiples of 500 and the +25 cap is **142,600**, a multiple of 100. Thus every `expAfter − expBefore` is divisible by 100 on such paths. The first-driver Tube-only subset avoids fractional credit arithmetic entirely, **provided no feed/recovery injects odd EXP**. It does not prove cash rounding for later recovery-backed transactions.

## B2 exit criterion and minimal fresh-client capture plan

**The existing B2 blocker remains OPEN.** Mark source findings independently `VERIFIED` rather than converting the whole behavior into a `VERIFIED` mechanic. Target a **Version 3.7+ client** (record exact date/platform/build) and record **before and after** resource balances plus upgrade-screen preview. Use a fresh Rank-5 Echo, record `expBefore` and `tunedThrough`, and isolate individual transactions:

1. **Intermediate carry:** `+0` → **one Premium 5,000-EXP Tube** to reach +5 eligibility plus 600 carry. Record the **upgrade-only Shell Credit quote**, actual debit, newly stored EXP, Tube decrement and (separately) the **+5 tune quote/debit**. Then spend to +10 using the 5,000 carried EXP, verify no duplicated charge.
2. **Exact threshold comparison:** Use the verified progression and a preparation that genuinely has **4,400 effective EXP**, if available; compare next 100/500 EXP quote/debit without inventing a fractional Tube. For Tube-only inputs, also test `9 Basic = 4,500` against `1 Premium = 5,000` with the same level threshold.
3. **+25 overflow:** Start at `+0`, preview/commit **72 Advanced = 144,000**. Record quoted and **actual 14,260 Shell Credit debit**, before/after Tube holdings for each denomination, exact cap EXP, **what happened to the 1,400 overflow**, and whether any automatic return is separately itemized. Reconcile total 144,000 = 142,600 + returnedTubeEXP + unrepresentedEXP using actual observations, not the engine's assumed largest-first decomposition.
4. **Rounding / non-Tube input (separate capability):** Only if actual feed/EXP sources can create a `expBefore` with a non-10 remainder, compare controlled increments that lead to fractional `0.1 × effective EXP`. Check actual Shell debit and displayed rounding with multiple examples, preserving raw video or sequential balance screenshots.
5. **Tuning & rollback:** Quote/commit each of the five tunes independently (expected **10 Tuners + 2,000 Credits** each, without hidden auto-tune). Confirm insufficient Shell Credits prevents spend and doesn't consume an Echo/Tube/Tuner; a replay/double-click test should preserve one actual debit, but transaction safety belongs to **B1 / BUG-045**, not B2.

**Stop rule:** If intermediate carry, exact-return receipts or rounding remain inaccessible, keep the corresponding behavior **PENDING**. A source table plus synthetic ledger is **not** enough to sign off universal B2 or S1. B3's **feed vs Data Recovery return receipts** are separately pending and **not** addressed by this Shell Credit study.
